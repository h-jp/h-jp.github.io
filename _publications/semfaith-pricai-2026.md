abstract:
  en: |
    Classical-to-modern Chinese restatement requires recovering implicit meanings and reorganizing compact classical expressions. Although large language models (LLMs) can generate fluent modern Chinese restatements, such outputs may still be semantically unfaithful to the source.

    We propose **SemFaith**, a checker-assisted claim-level diagnostic framework for semantic faithfulness in this setting. For each anchor, SemFaith derives anchor-licensed consensus claims from multiple human translations or authoritative explanations, aligns generated claims with those consensus claims, and computes interpretable preservation, omission, semantic-shift, and unsupported-expansion rates.

    We construct ACRE from corrected *Lunyu* and *Mencius* records, yielding 961 anchors, 2,744 anchor-licensed consensus claims, and 2,883 restatements generated by Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, Yi-1.5-9B-Chat, and GLM-4-9B-Chat. Under a completed Qwen2.5-7B checker setting, SemFaith reveals substantial checker-conditioned differences in faithfulness loss; these measurements remain subject to future human calibration.

    The results show that fluent restatements can still omit source-licensed claims, shift semantic relations, or introduce unsupported expansions, highlighting the need for claim-level diagnosis beyond surface similarity.
  zh: |
    将文言文重述为现代汉语，需要还原隐含语义，并重新组织凝练的文言表达。尽管大语言模型能够生成流畅的现代汉语重述，其语义仍可能不忠实于原文。

    我们提出 **SemFaith**，一个借助检查器、针对这一任务开展主张级语义忠实性诊断的框架。对于每个原文锚点，SemFaith 从多份人工译文或权威阐释中提取由该锚点支持的共识主张，将生成文本中的主张与这些共识主张对齐，并计算可解释的保留率、遗漏率、语义偏移率和无依据扩展率。

    我们基于经过校订的《论语》和《孟子》记录构建 ACRE，其中包含 961 个锚点、2,744 条由锚点支持的共识主张，以及由 Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct、Yi-1.5-9B-Chat 和 GLM-4-9B-Chat 生成的 2,883 条重述。在已完成的 Qwen2.5-7B 检查器设置下，SemFaith 揭示了语义忠实性损失的较大差异；这些测量结果受检查器设置影响，仍有待后续人工校准。

    结果表明，即使重述流畅，也可能遗漏原文支持的主张、改变语义关系，或引入无依据的扩展。因此，有必要开展超越表面相似度的主张级诊断。
